# Licensing and attribution

## Analysis code

The original analysis and verification code in this repository is released
under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.

## Source and derived data

The analytical source is the Our World in Data Energy dataset. Its codebook
documents provider-level lineage, including Ember's Yearly Electricity Data and
the Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy. Source and derived
data retain the attribution and reuse conditions of their upstream providers.
Users should consult the current [OWID data-reuse guidance](https://ourworldindata.org/how-to-use-our-world-in-data)
and the provider lineage in the OWID codebook before redistribution.

The derived panel is supplied to make the reported analysis independently
auditable and reproducible. No ownership claim is made over third-party source
measurements.
